# Lab photo credits

The Lina Haddad lab pages (a made-up street photographer in Beirut) use placeholder photos of Beirut and Lebanon from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/). Each file is marked **public domain** or **CC0** there, so no attribution is required; the details are listed here anyway. They are demo content, not Yusra's own photos, and are not used anywhere else on the site.

56 source photos. The files in `lina/with/` and `lina/without/` are crops and resized copies of these (some sources appear twice, as a tighter detail crop). Licence labels are copied from each file's Commons page; check the page if it matters for your use.
